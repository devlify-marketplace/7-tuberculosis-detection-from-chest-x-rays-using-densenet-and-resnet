Tuberculosis-associated CXR screening — research demo

Compare DenseNet121 and ResNet50 checkpoints trained on the public NLM chest X-ray collections.

Not a medical device. This experimental classifier is not validated for clinical use, does not diagnose tuberculosis, and must not be used to make health decisions. Clinical assessment and confirmatory testing are essential.

Files are processed in memory for this request and are not written to disk. The score is an uncalibrated model output, not a probability of disease.

Dataset task: normal vs. abnormal radiographs with TB-associated readings in the source collection. Public benchmark data are limited and are not representative of all patients, scanners, or clinical settings.
